# Theme Toggle

Switches the app between light and dark mode. Available both as the sun/moon icon in the top bar
and as a "Switch to light/dark mode" entry in the hamburger menu — the same action, two entry
points.

Your choice is remembered per-browser and applies immediately across the whole app, not just the
canvas.
